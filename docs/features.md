# Features

## Mission Control

Create, rename, edit, and delete polling sessions. Build single-select and multi-select questions with an optional answer key. Questions stay in draft until the host starts voting. Reset the lobby, clear one question's responses or every response, and return all questions to draft when a session needs a fresh run.

## Voter experience

Voters join through a session link or QR code, choose their name and avatar, and appear in the shared lobby. Answer labels stay masked until voting opens. Selections save automatically and can be revised until close. Closed questions reveal correct choices, answer totals, and voter pills.

## Presenter experience

Use first, previous, next, and last navigation. Start voting without a time limit, or use a 15-, 30-, 45-, or 60-second countdown. Stop immediately, clear responses, and choose when to show session results. The join link and QR code remain available on the display.

## Results

Questions with answer keys produce a leaderboard with shared ranks for ties. Multi-select answers require the full correct selection. Opinion-only polls show groups of at most three people connected by similar responses.

## PowerPoint and local preview

A browser wrapper can load the presenter view in an embedded frame. The rebuild also includes an on-device preview for trying the complete workflow in several tabs of the same browser. A connected backend is required for people joining from separate devices.
